Who we are

About Us

SmartSpin is a student-built project focused on making laundry-related tasks easier, cleaner, and more user-friendly through a modern app experience.

Our Goal

We want to build a smart platform that improves how users interact with laundry tools and support features.

Our Design

We focus on simple navigation, modern visuals, and a layout that is easy to present and expand.

Future Vision

SmartSpin can grow into a platform with AI chat, image-based help, and personalized account experiences.

Why SmartSpin?

  • Cleaner experience than a basic student prototype
  • Flexible structure for backend integration
  • Easy to demonstrate during project meetings
  • Consistent design across all pages
